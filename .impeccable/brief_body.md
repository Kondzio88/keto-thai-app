STORY: The user sees exactly what tools they get to win, presented as professional locker-room documentation.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A massive bento grid showing Video Analysis, 24/7 Mentor Contact, Daily Targets, and Resources. No long scrolling text above the fold.
FORM: The Deliverables Grid (Option 4).
